Inverse Puzzle

Drag the pieces on top of each other so the workspace matches the target exactly. Overlapping colors mix additively (Red + Green = Yellow, Green + Blue = Cyan, …). A plain click (no dragging) on a piece in the tray selects it, so you can rotate/flip it. While dragging, or with a piece selected: R rotates, F flips, Esc cancels the drag.
